SOLO BI LEAD · 90-DAY PLAN

Establish trust before scale.

A practical sequence for a regulated bank: reduce decision risk now, create repeatable governance next, and prepare the platform decision with evidence.

Days 0–30Foundation

Align, inventory, and publish the first trusted layer.

  • Run 12 stakeholder interviews; map decisions, owners, and conflicting KPIs.
  • Stand up the KPI council and certify the top 15 metrics.
  • Ship this executive scorecard with lineage, freshness, and access controls.
  • Profile critical source data; log quality risks and reconciliation gaps.
Outcome: one trusted executive view and a governed metric backlog.
Days 31–60Repeatability

Turn definitions into a durable service.

  • Prioritise a 3-mart semantic layer: customer, credit portfolio, finance.
  • Implement data tests for completeness, freshness, uniqueness, and GL reconciliation.
  • Publish role-based self-service access and intake / SLA workflow.
  • Train analysts on certified versus exploratory usage.
Outcome: recurring reporting no longer depends on manual reconciliation.
Days 61–90Scale

Prove adoption and choose the right platform path.

  • Instrument adoption and time-to-insight; retire duplicate reports.
  • Expand certified KPIs to collections, retention, and unit economics.
  • Run a targeted BI tool evaluation against security, lineage, and semantic needs.
  • Submit a staffed 6-month roadmap with quantified data debt and ROI.
Outcome: an evidence-backed path from prototype to enterprise BI.
